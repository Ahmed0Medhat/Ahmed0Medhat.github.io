WHATSII

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 10, 2026

Whatsii lets you open a WhatsApp chat without adding the recipient to your contacts. Recent numbers, favorites, and message templates stay on your device. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives this content. The app displays ads through Google AdMob, which processes certain device and usage data as described below.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to Whatsii – Without Saving Number, package com.ahmedmedhat.whatsii.launcher, developed by Ahmed Medhat. Questions can be sent to a7mdmedhat2011@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter or share

  • Phone numbers and messages: the app processes the number and optional message you enter to create a WhatsApp or WhatsApp Web link.
  • Share menu: on Android, text you deliberately share to Whatsii is processed to fill the phone-number or message field.
  • Clipboard: Whatsii reads clipboard text only after you tap the Paste button.
  • QR scanning: the camera is used only when you open the QR scanner. Whatsii does not save camera images or video. The decoded QR text is used to fill the relevant field.

This content is processed on the device. We do not receive it on a developer-operated server. When you choose to open a chat, the selected phone number and message are passed to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Web, whose handling of that information is governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.

3. Information stored on your device

Whatsii can store the following locally when you use the related features:

  • recently used phone numbers and the selected WhatsApp app;
  • phone numbers you mark as favorites; and
  • message templates you explicitly save.

These items remain on your device until you remove them in the app, clear Whatsii's app data, or uninstall the app. They are not synced to a Whatsii account or developer server.

4. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Whatsii uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner advertising. According to Google's SDK disclosure, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Where required, Whatsii uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before requesting ads and to provide a Privacy choices entry point. Advertising data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Policies and Technologies. You can also manage advertising controls through your device and Google account settings.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: required for advertising and the WhatsApp Web fallback.
  • Camera: requested only when you use the QR scanner.

Whatsii does not request access to contacts, precise or coarse device location, microphone, call logs, SMS, or notification permissions. Google may infer approximate location from the device IP address for advertising without Whatsii requesting Android location permission.

6. Data sharing and international processing

We do not sell your user-entered phone numbers or messages. Information may be processed by Google for advertising and by WhatsApp when you initiate a chat, as explained above. These providers may process information in countries other than your own under their respective privacy policies and legal safeguards.

7. Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Delete individual favorites, recent entries, or templates using the controls in the app.
  • Use the available clear actions for recent numbers and message templates.
  • Clear the app's storage or uninstall Whatsii to remove all locally saved Whatsii data.
  • Use Privacy choices in the app when shown to review advertising consent options.

Whatsii has no user accounts and therefore has no account record to delete from a developer server. For data controlled by Google or WhatsApp, use the controls and contact methods described in their privacy policies.

8. Security

We limit app permissions and keep saved Whatsii content in the app's local storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and third-party services are responsible for the security of data they process.

9. Children's privacy

Whatsii is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us directly, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
