Privacy Policy for Phone Test Pro

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Ahmed Medhat ("we", "us") provides Phone Test Pro. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Diagnostic information

The app accesses device APIs only for diagnostics you choose. These may include sensor readings, battery and device information, network and Bluetooth state, NFC, camera, microphone, flashlight, vibration, and precise location.

  • Raw sensor readings and location coordinates are displayed while the relevant screen is open. They are not stored in the saved diagnostic summary.
  • The local summary contains only a test identifier, status, short description, and timestamp.
  • Camera captures are deleted immediately. Microphone samples are temporary and deleted when replaced, confirmed, or when you leave the screen.
  • PDF reports are generated locally in temporary storage and shared only with a destination you choose.
  • You can clear saved results in the app. Android app backup is disabled.

The app does not send diagnostic results to an AI service or to our servers.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob banner ads and optional rewarded ads. After the free diagnostic allowance is used, you may choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock unlimited diagnostics for 15 minutes. Google Mobile Ads may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, secure, and personalize ads where permitted.

Google's User Messaging Platform is used to request and respect required privacy choices before ads are requested. Where required, choices can be reopened from Settings. Premium users do not receive ads.

Purchases

Google Play processes subscriptions and payment details. The app receives purchase status and keeps a local premium-status cache. We do not receive full payment-card details. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Retention, children, and choices

Local summaries remain until cleared, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Google retains advertising and purchase data under its policies. The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13.

You may revoke permissions, clear diagnostic results, manage ad privacy choices where available, manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play, or uninstall the app.
